The Drop

Once a day, at a time nobody knows in advance, the window opens for two minutes. If your team is mid-project, photograph the work and bank a bonus. If you're not doing anything, you can't fake it.

Checking the window…

Rear camera only. Photograph the work — the table, the flyers, the bookmarks, hands writing cards.

Why there is no selfie here. BeReal's signature move is the front camera. On a campaign about eating-disorder awareness, a schoolwide feed of student faces invites exactly the appearance comparison the campaign exists to reduce. So the front camera is disabled, the required shot is the work rather than the people, there are no comments — only reactions — and every Drop routes to an advisor before it reaches the feed.

What this form will never ask for. There is no free-text box anywhere in this app, and no field for personal experiences, diagnoses, weight, food intake, or health information. The database is built so that information cannot be entered — the only things it can hold are teams, approved actions, hours, and timestamps.

Advisor review
